The core problem

Strategic plans fail atthe same point every time.

Six weeks after the offsite
The planning session goes well. Then the document goes into a shared folder. Then the day-to-day takes over.
By Q2 the team is executing on instinct. By Q3 nobody can remember what the top three priorities were without opening a 40-slide deck. By Q4 there's a retrospective about why the year didn't go to plan — and then another offsite to produce another strategy document. The problem isn't the planning. It's that strategy lives in a document instead of in a system.
